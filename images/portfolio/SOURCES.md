# Portfolio asset sources

The Portfolio page is the single complete artwork destination. The homepage contains linked previews, not separate project statements. Titles and artwork information follow Ke Li's supplied portfolios.

## Archive attribution

- Eden: Jewellery & Metal Portfolio, 2023–2024 (PRP KeLi.pdf / PRP KeLi.key), pages 4, 14, 18, and 25. The date range follows the original portfolio cover.
- Shape of Traveling Memory: LIKE - RCA.pdf, pages 7–11, dated 2022. The excerpt comes from page 8.
- Psychotherapy: LIKE - RCA.pdf, pages 1–3, dated 2022. The personal health narrative is omitted.
- Symbiotic relief: LIKE - RCA.pdf, pages 4–6, dated 2022. The excerpt comes from page 4. The user confirmed that Breaking and Symbiotic relief are the same series and requested a folder-led reconstruction. The website now consistently follows this original portfolio's title, 2022 date, and material labels; it does not present Breaking as a separate 2026 series.
- Excerpts retain the original English. Line breaks, extracted PDF ligatures, and quotation marks are normalized for HTML; no new artist statement is generated.
- Reference imagery by other artists, administrative records, and full source documents are not published.
